Our Fellowship

A Church Rooted
in God's Love

We are a Brethren Church located in Kempapura, Hebbal and Yelahanka — helping our fellow Christian Brothers and Sisters to grow Spiritually in the Lord.

Currently we meet as a Telugu congregation. We break communion every week and worship as per the New Testament Doctrine which has been handed to us through the Apostles who were led by the Power of the Holy Spirit on the day of Pentecost.

Please join us to grow spiritually and in Word as per the scriptures and being transformed into the image of Our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
